What our articles are for

The Art World Report explains how the art trade works. Our articles are general information, not financial, legal, tax or investment advice. Art prices can go down as well as up, and a past auction result does not tell you what a work will fetch in future. Before you buy, sell or donate a work of art, take advice from someone who knows your circumstances.

Accuracy

We take every fact and figure from a published source and link to it. Sources can be wrong or change, and auction houses sometimes revise results or rates. Each article shows the date it was written. If you find a mistake, please tell us and we will look into it.

Where we report an auction price we say whether it is the hammer price or includes the buyer's premium, and where we have not checked a figure against the auction house's own records we say that too.
