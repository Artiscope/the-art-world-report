Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This page explains what happens to information about you when you visit The Art World Report. The short version: we do not run analytics, we do not use advertising cookies, and we do not ask you for personal details.

Who we are

The Art World Report is published at theartworldreport.com. You can reach us at info@grovegallery.com.

What the site itself collects

Nothing. The site is a set of plain web pages. It has no accounts, no comments and no tracking code, and it does not set cookies.

The newsletter form on the Briefing page is not connected to any mailing service yet. If you type an email address into it, the address stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere. We will update this page before sign-up opens.

Services the site relies on

Three outside services are involved when a page loads. Each one receives the technical details that any website receives, such as your IP address and browser type.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may keep server logs for security and to run the service. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. See Google's notes on Fonts and privacy.
  • jsDelivr supplies a small script that makes scrolling smoother. See the jsDelivr privacy policy. If it does not load, the site works as normal.

If you email us

We will see your email address and anything you include in the message. We use it only to reply, and we do not add you to any list.

Links to other sites

Our articles link to their sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and what happens there is up to them.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EU, data protection law gives you rights over personal information held about you, including the right to see it and to have it deleted. As we do not collect anything through the site, in practice this applies only to emails you have sent us. Write to the address above and we will deal with it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
