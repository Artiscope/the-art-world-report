About

Understanding the Business of Art.

The Art World Report is about the business side of art: galleries, auction houses, fairs, and the money that moves between them. We write for collectors, artists and anyone else who wants to know how the trade works.

How we work

Sourced

Every fact and figure comes from a published source, and we link to it in the article and list it at the end.

Nothing invented

We do not make up quotes, interviews, examples or statistics. When we could not find something out, we say so.

Prices as reported

Auction prices keep their original currency, and we say whether a figure is the hammer price or includes the buyer's premium.

Who is behind a figure

When a figure comes from someone with an interest in it, such as a consultancy that sells the service it measures, we point that out.

Not advice

We explain how the market works. We do not tell anyone what to buy or sell.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, tell us. We will check it and put it right.
